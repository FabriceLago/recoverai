# Product

## Positioning

RecoverAI detects the sales opportunities about to slip away and tells your team what to do next. The proof metric is **Revenue Recovered**.

## The loop

Detect an at-risk opportunity, explain why (score), recommend an action, draft the follow-up, act, get a reply, mark recovered, measure.

## Screens

- **Landing:** pitch, pricing, FAQ.
- **Onboarding:** create the workspace; start with simulation, CSV, or empty.
- **Revenue Radar:** Pipeline, Revenue at Risk, Actions Today, Revenue Recovered, and a "Needs Attention" list.
- **Opportunities:** search, filter, sort, paginate. The detail page has risk analysis, recommended action, suggested email, timeline and mark-as-recovered.
- **Action Center:** Today, Upcoming, Completed.
- **Import:** CSV upload, validation, preview, import.
- **Settings:** integrations, simulation, billing.

## Definition of done (user journey)

Sign up, create an organization, activate simulation, see about 20 opportunities, open the Revenue Radar, open one opportunity, understand its score, see the action, generate a follow-up, edit it, simulate the send, mark it recovered, and watch Revenue Recovered increase.
